# ADR-039: Ledger schema v2 with unique reconciliation ids

Decision: move the ledger to schema v2, which enforces a unique `recon_id`, and migrate history with a
**full backfill** instead of dual writes.
Consequence: every historical duplicate reconciliation id must be cleaned up before the backfill can succeed.
