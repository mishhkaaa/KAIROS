# ADR-042: Dual-run reconciliation

Decision: keep the legacy pipeline running until the new one matches it for 14 consecutive days.
Consequence: roughly doubles reconciliation cloud cost while both run.

## Decommissioning the legacy pipeline

The legacy reconciliation pipeline is decommissioned (switched off) once the new pipeline has matched it for
14 consecutive days; that switch-off is tracked as [APOLLO-18](../jira/apollo-18.md), and the parity status is in the
[reconciliation parity report](../engineering/reconciliation-parity-report.md). Until then the dual-run, and its
doubled cloud cost, continues.
