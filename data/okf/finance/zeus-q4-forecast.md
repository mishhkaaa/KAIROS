# Zeus Q4 forecast and budget risk

Project Zeus budget risk: the Q4 forecast is 5.4 lakh against a Q4 budget of 4.0 lakh, a projected overrun of 1.4 lakh (35%).
Drivers: +0.7 lakh from the Cumulus warehouse renewal (compute credit price up 40% from 1 November);
+0.4 lakh because ad-hoc query volume doubled with no per-team query budgets;
+0.3 lakh for a contractor extension to finish the cost guardrails (ZEUS-9).

| Line item | Q4 budget (lakh) | Q4 forecast (lakh) |
|---|---|---|
| Warehouse compute | 2.0 | 3.1 |
| Engineering | 2.0 | 2.3 |
| Total | 4.0 | 5.4 |

Zeus closed Q3 on budget (see the Zeus budget sheet); the risk is entirely in Q4. Tracked in ZEUS-11.
