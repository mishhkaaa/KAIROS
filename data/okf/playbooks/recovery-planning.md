# Recovery planning playbook

Use this when a project is late or over budget. A recovery plan has five sections:

1. **Situation:** schedule slip and budget variance, with evidence links.
2. **Root causes:** each backed by at least two independent documents.
3. **Options:** at least two, each with cost, time and risk.
4. **Recommendation:** owner and dates for each action.
5. **Approvals needed:** see the [approval policy](../policies/approvals.md).

Update the tracker issue with the root causes and link the plan.
