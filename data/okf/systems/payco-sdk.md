# PayCo SDK

Client library for the PayCo payment processor, integrated by Marco Rossi's team.

- **v4:** in production; supported until 2027-03-31.
- **v5:** required by the new reconciliation pipeline ([ADR-045](../decisions/ADR-045.md)); GA delayed by PayCo's
  PCI certification. Upgrade tracked in [APOLLO-31](../jira/apollo-31.md).
