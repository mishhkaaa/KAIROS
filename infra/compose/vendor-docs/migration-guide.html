Migrating from SDK v4 to v5

Status: SDK v5 general availability is scheduled for 2026-10-20, pending PCI certification. Until GA, production traffic must stay on v4.

Recommended path

  1. Keep v4 in production; v4 is supported until 2027-03-31.
  2. Run v4 and v5 pipelines in parallel in staging and compare settlement and reconciliation output.
  3. Cut over only after GA and after your own parity checks pass.

Support

Customers who need hands-on migration help before GA can buy an emergency support contract.
