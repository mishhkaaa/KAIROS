PayCo SDK v5: release status

Updated 2026-09-15. General availability of SDK v5 has moved to 2026-10-20 because of an outstanding PCI certification issue.

Impact for customers migrating from v4

  • v4 remains supported until 2027-03-31.
  • Customers on the early-access programme should keep dual-running v4 and v5 pipelines until GA.
  • Emergency migration support is available under a paid support contract.
