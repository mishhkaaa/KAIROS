Cumulus Data: warehouse pricing update

Updated 2026-09-22. For contracts renewing on or after 2026-11-01, the compute credit price rises by 40%.

How customers can limit the impact

  • Per-team query budgets cap spend before it is billed.
  • Moving hourly dashboard refreshes to daily typically halves compute use.
  • A one-year commitment signed before renewal keeps the current rate for the first quarter.
